Chang Scan · Document Scanner

Technical Support

Need help with Chang Scan? Try the steps below or email us. Include your device model and iOS version, and describe the steps that led to the issue. Please do not email sensitive documents.

Scan a document

Open the camera scanner, frame the page using the edge guides, tap to focus if needed, then press the shutter yourself. Review the captured page and use Save to add it to your document library.

Import from Photos

From the document library, choose the photo import action and select the images in the iOS picker. Keep the app open until processing finishes.

Find recognized text

Open a saved document and use its text recognition or search tools. Recognition runs on the device; results can vary with image quality, handwriting, and language.

Back up or restore

Use the backup action in the document library to save a package to Files or, after confirming the warning, to Chang Scan’s iCloud Drive container. Choose the corresponding restore action and select a backup. iCloud Drive must be enabled on the device. Backups are not encrypted by the app and may include vault documents.

Contact support

Email stark.888889@gmail.com. We will review your request and reply when possible; response times are not guaranteed.
