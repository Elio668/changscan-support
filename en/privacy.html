Chang Scan · Document Scanner

Privacy Policy

Chang Scan is designed to process your scans on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens when you choose to back up or share documents.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Information the app handles

The app processes scan images imported from your camera or photo picker, OCR text, document names and organization details you enter, and content you add such as annotations, signatures, and watermark text. Business-card details are recognized from an image and shown for your review.

On-device processing and permissions

Scanning, image editing, and text recognition use Apple’s on-device system frameworks. Camera access is requested when you use the camera. Photos are selected through the iOS photo picker. Chang Scan does not read your existing contacts; if you choose to save a recognized business card, iOS presents its Contacts editor for you to review and confirm.

No developer account or analytics service

Chang Scan does not require an account and does not send your documents or OCR text to a server operated by the developer. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Optional iCloud Drive backup

If you explicitly create an iCloud backup, the app writes a backup package to its iCloud Drive container so Apple can sync it with your iCloud account. The package can include scan images, OCR data, document details, and documents in the in-app vault. The backup package is not encrypted by Chang Scan. Anyone who can access that iCloud content may be able to read it. Use your Apple Account security settings and delete backup files from iCloud Drive when you no longer need them. iCloud is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Exporting and sharing

When you export or share a document, iOS sends the selected content to the destination you choose, such as Files, Mail, or another app. The recipient or service then handles the data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Storage, deletion, and security

Documents are stored in the app’s local storage unless you export or back them up. You can delete documents in the app. Deleting an in-app document does not delete a separate export or iCloud backup; remove those copies from their destination as well. iOS sandboxing helps protect local app data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above shows the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact us at stark.888889@gmail.com.
